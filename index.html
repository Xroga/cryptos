About OfCrypto

Finance, rewritten
as a public good

OfCrypto is a next-generation decentralized finance protocol built on a proof-of-stake settlement layer. It lets anyone — anywhere — hold, swap, lend, and earn digital assets without relying on a bank, broker, or custodian.

Launched by a global collective of engineers and researchers, OfCrypto combines audited smart contracts, a distributed validator network, and an intent-based routing engine to deliver financial primitives that are open, composable, and built to last.

  • ✓ Non-custodial by design — you always hold your keys
  • ✓ Open-source, audited, and formally verified
  • ✓ Carbon-neutral consensus since day one
